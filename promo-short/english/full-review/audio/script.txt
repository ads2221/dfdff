## c00 Intro
Every day, whales on Solana launch DCA orders worth tens of thousands of dollars. What if you could get in right alongside them — and get out exactly when the whale is done buying? This is DCA Solana Trade, and the site just got a complete redesign. Today we'll cover everything: how the bot works, real monitoring numbers, the whole dashboard, plans and fees, and the Telegram mini app. Let's go!
## c01 Home page
A quick look at the home page. Enter with the whale, exit on time — that's the whole idea. The bot sees whale DCA orders on Solana, filters them by your rules, buys into your wallet, and sells when the whale is finished. Below that you'll find live monitoring results and slots with their stats, which you can check even before you sign up.
## c03 Getting started
Signing up takes an email, a username and a password, you confirm with a code from your inbox, and a Solana wallet is created automatically. Right away you land on the new dashboard with a First Steps checklist: fund your wallet, choose a slot, set up filters, turn on auto-trading, connect Telegram, and enable two-factor. Steps tick themselves off, and the dashboard tour shows you where everything is. Below that: wallet capital, total PnL with a curve, your slot, active whale orders and quick actions.
## c04 Wallet
The Wallet page shows your address, your SOL and USDC balances, and a deposit window. The Trading readiness block tells you right away what's missing: SOL covers network fees, USDC funds the trades themselves. Only send funds on the Solana network.
## c05 DCA V3 monitoring
The heart of the platform is DCA V3 monitoring. Over a thousand orders, the win rate, and total PnL across everything closed. Here's the cool part: every order links to DEX Chart, Birdeye and GMGN, so you can check it yourself. Orders filter by status and sort by PnL, market cap, liquidity and volume. Each card shows the DCA size, cycle progress, market cap and the result. On the free plan, entry and exit prices are locked behind PRO.
## c06 DCA stats
DCA Stats shows how closed orders performed over a week, a month, three months or all time: the share of winners, profit factor, drawdown, a daily curve and the distribution. Important: these are whale orders, not your trades — the bot enters a bit later, plus fees and slippage.
## c07 Filter lab
Next up, the filter lab. Set the period, trade size, number of positions, take-profit and stop-loss, turn on costs — and the run uses the exact same code the bot uses to decide whether to enter. On the right you instantly see the net total, monthly result, win rate, drawdown and profit factor. Like it? One click moves the set into your filters.
## c08 Trading and filters
On the Trading page, a new block called What the bot will do explains your current setup in plain words. Then there's the master switch, buy amount, closing together with the whale, slippage, and execution through Jupiter. The Filters page now has a forecast: what your filters would have made over the last thirty days, and which ones cut the most. Plus take-profit, stop-loss and trailing for all positions, and a blacklist for tokens and traders.
## c09 How the bot decides
So how does the bot decide whether to buy? The checks run in a strict order: subscription, blacklist, repeat token, your filters, slot, amount, balance, and price impact. The first one that fails stops the buy, and Telegram tells you why. Exits: the whale completes the order, the whale cancels — then it always sells — take-profit or stop-loss, or manually. If the network rejects a sell, the bot retries, but it never sells twice.
## c10 Plans and fees
Plans. Free is watch-only, with a fifteen-minute delay. PRO runs for twelve months, and the price depends on your slot — the size of the orders you copy. Basic covers orders up to nine thousand dollars, for three hundred a year. Pro is nine to thirty-five thousand, for six hundred. Unlimited is any size, for twelve hundred. Spots are limited. The fee is twenty percent, and only on profit. Put in five hundred, sell for five sixty — the fee is twelve dollars, you keep forty-eight. Losing trades are never charged.

Portfolio shows open positions, what's invested and your PnL, while My Stats shows your real trades. Your profile has two-factor protection, active sessions, and a button to replay the tutorial.
